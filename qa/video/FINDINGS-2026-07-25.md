   length ratio 1.000. Do not re-suspect the Swift sampler.

   cfg): 0.106 vs ship 0.17. Each departure (comp33: E, cfg321: D) degrades it back
   toward 0.15.

   SAME schedule — while our 8-ladder ship config reaches ~60 through the same
   upsampler/refine/decode.

   3-step refine fails to produce detail from a smoothly-converged (20-step) latent,
   while ComfyUI's does. The 8-ladder's aggressive tail (0.725→0.42→0) leaves
   high-frequency energy that masks the deficiency.

   regardless: src_hist_corr 0.883 → 0.92–0.93.
