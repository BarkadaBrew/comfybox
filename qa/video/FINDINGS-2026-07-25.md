## Established facts

1. **Stage-1 sampler parity is PROVEN.** Per-step trajectory dumps (LTX2_TRAJ_DUMP +
   ComfyUI to_d patch) show gen-token deltas matching ComfyUI to 0.1%, total path
   length ratio 1.000. Do not re-suspect the Swift sampler.
2. **Reversal coherence lever = the GT stage-1 config** (20-step sigmas + comp 2 + flat
   cfg): 0.106 vs ship 0.17. Each departure (comp33: E, cfg321: D) degrades it back
   toward 0.15.
3. **Sharpness is NOT explained by**: cfg schedule (D), compression (E), distill bake
   (B partial: +13), refine anchor detail (C), output bitrate (ours 2× GT's).
   With 20-step sigmas our final sharpness is stuck at 20–35 vs ComfyUI's 58.8 on the
   SAME schedule — while our 8-ladder ship config reaches ~60 through the same
   upsampler/refine/decode.
4. **Therefore the sharpness loss is downstream of stage 1** — upsampler and/or
   3-step refine fails to produce detail from a smoothly-converged (20-step) latent,
   while ComfyUI's does. The 8-ladder's aggressive tail (0.725→0.42→0) leaves
   high-frequency energy that masks the deficiency.
5. Refine re-anchor to native-res source (commit 3cec6a9) is a real identity win
   regardless: src_hist_corr 0.883 → 0.92–0.93.

## Evening session (#36 completion + Codex consult): the breakthroughs

1. **Exact streaming VAE decode landed** (9501f4c): bit-exact chunked-io port;
   also proved the PLAIN conv call itself silently miscomputes boundary frames
   at large spatial extents (MLX int32-offset class) — the "plain reference"
   used in earlier comparisons was corrupt.
2. **Conditioning CRF mapping** (3981a68): img_compression is libx264 CRF in
   ComfyUI; our proxy was 14x too lossy at low values.
3. **Workflow-exact conditioning geometry** (46dca39): center-crop (not
   stretch) + crop->resize->compress ordering.
4. **Temporal RoPE in SECONDS** (46dca39, via Codex consult): ComfyUI divides
   temporal coords by frame_rate before RoPE; ours had it removed. Without it,
   coords exceeded trained max_pos 20 by 2.4x and frames sat 24x further apart
   than trained — frames rendered near-independently. THE shimmer + frenetic/
   static-motion root cause across BOTH modes.
