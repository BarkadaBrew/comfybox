# Codex review — LTX-2 audio wires (2026-08-04)

Scope: 9 audio commits (block parity → API). Full transcript replaced by this distillation.
All 10 findings folded same-day; see commit "fix(audio): fold Codex review".

1. **[BLOCKER] Cross-modal video RoPE divides temporal coordinates by FPS twice.** The production grid already converts time to seconds using `condFps ?? fps` in [LTX2Pipeline.swift:1587](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/LTX2/LTX2Pipeline.swift:1587), then [LTX2TransformerAV.swift:115](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/LTX2/Transformer/LTX2TransformerAV.swift:115) divides those values by `frameRate` again. The reference performs exactly one division in [av_model.py:867](/Volumes/Bolt/ComfyUI-validate/ComfyUI/comfy/ldm/lightricks/av_model.py:867). At 24fps, roughly four seconds of video are presented to A/V cross-attention as about 0.16 seconds while audio remains in real seconds, breaking synchronization. `condFps != output fps` makes the unit mismatch even less recoverable.

2. **[BLOCKER] Admission control is unaware that an audio warm-key change unloads and rebuilds with another ~11.3GiB.** Audio participates in the generator key and causes a reload at [LTX2VideoGenerator.swift:307](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/LTX2/LTX2VideoGenerator.swift:307), but admission considers any loaded generator “warm” and asks for only 24GiB at [WarmServer.swift:5363](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/Server/WarmServer.swift:5363). Cold int8 requests still use the audio-blind 40GiB estimate in [HeavyModelAdmission.swift:40](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/Server/HeavyModelAdmission.swift:40). A video-only stack resident with 24–35GiB available can therefore be admitted, torn down, and rebuilt with the BF16 audio branch plus FP32 codec, ending in Metal OOM/jetsam. Alternating audio/silent requests also unnecessarily rebuilds in both directions.

3. **[MAJOR] AV conditioning forces the production BF16 joint stream into float32.** All audio and cross-modal AdaLN calls hard-code `hiddenDtype: .float32` at [LTX2TransformerAV.swift:60](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/LTX2/Transformer/LTX2TransformerAV.swift:60), while the reference passes the model input dtype at [av_model.py:803](/Volumes/Bolt/ComfyUI-validate/ComfyUI/comfy/ldm/lightricks/av_model.py:803). Production transformer inputs are BF16 at [LTX2Pipeline.swift:1027](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/LTX2/LTX2Pipeline.swift:1027); MLX promotes BF16 mixed with float32 to float32 ([dtype.cpp:48](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/.build/checkouts/mlx-swift/Source/Cmlx/mlx/mlx/dtype.cpp:48)). The first audio/cross modulation therefore promotes activations and subsequently both streams, increasing activation memory and diverging from reference numerics.

4. **[MAJOR] Unsupported extended audio renders succeed with only the first chunk’s audio.** Validation does not reject audio with continuation chunks at [LTX2VideoGenerator.swift:262](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/LTX2/LTX2VideoGenerator.swift:262). Only chunk zero receives `audioSeconds` at [LTX2VideoGenerator.swift:898](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/LTX2/LTX2VideoGenerator.swift:898), but its latents are retained and muxed over every accumulated frame at [LTX2VideoGenerator.swift:980](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/LTX2/LTX2VideoGenerator.swift:980). Durations above the 289-frame fold threshold therefore produce a successful MP4 whose audio ends after the first chunk. I2V with `audio:true` is similarly accepted and silently rendered without audio; known unsupported modes should be rejected until implemented.

5. **[MAJOR] A writer or append failure can deadlock the render queue indefinitely.** `startWriting()` and both append return values are ignored at [LTX2PostProcess.swift:334](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/LTX2/LTX2PostProcess.swift:334), [LTX2PostProcess.swift:373](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/LTX2/LTX2PostProcess.swift:373), and [LTX2PostProcess.swift:392](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/LTX2/LTX2PostProcess.swift:392). If the writer fails—for example, disk full or codec rejection—`isReadyForMoreMediaData` can remain false and neither callback executes `group.leave()`, while [LTX2PostProcess.swift:398](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/LTX2/LTX2PostProcess.swift:398) waits forever. The test run also emitted a priority-inversion warning at this wait.

6. **[MAJOR] Wire-4 is only partially exposed.** The direct local REST decoder supports `audio`, but the MCP schema has no property at [MCPToolRegistry.swift:421](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/MCP/MCPToolRegistry.swift:421), and its executor never forwards one at [MCPToolExecutor.swift:512](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/MCP/MCPToolExecutor.swift:512). The public endpoint request type lacks it at [VideoTypes.swift:34](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/Video/VideoTypes.swift:34), and the Tier-A registry has no audio field or `LTX2_AUDIO` fallback at [LTX2ConfigResolver.swift:217](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/LTX2/LTX2ConfigResolver.swift:217). `has_audio` is present in the trace, but the specified decode duration is not recorded in [WarmServer.swift:2251](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/Server/WarmServer.swift:2251).

7. **[MAJOR] Transformer and codec binding still accepts materially incomplete checkpoints.** The transformer only rejects coverage below 50% at [LTX2VideoGenerator.swift:417](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/LTX2/LTX2VideoGenerator.swift:417); missing AV modules below that threshold remain initialized defaults while the render proceeds. The audio VAE uses the same half-coverage rule at [LTX2AudioVAE.swift:389](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/LTX2/VAE/LTX2AudioVAE.swift:389). A truncated or differently-versioned monolith can therefore produce noise, silence, or corrupt joint video instead of failing the exact expected-key manifest requirement.

8. **[MINOR] Generated audio is never trimmed or padded to the actual video duration.** Audio length is quantized through `ceil(seconds*25)` at [LTX2Pipeline.swift:273](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/LTX2/LTX2Pipeline.swift:273), then becomes `4T−3` mel frames at [LTX2AudioVAE.swift:453](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/LTX2/VAE/LTX2AudioVAE.swift:453) and 480 output samples per mel frame at [LTX2Vocoder.swift:456](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/LTX2/LTX2Vocoder.swift:456). For the legal request 97 frames at 90fps, video is 1.0778s but audio is 1.05s—2.5 video frames short. The mux also accepts zero-length PCM without an explicit validation or trim policy.

9. **[MINOR] Unseeded direct pipeline calls reuse the same audio noise.** `generateT2V` exposes an optional seed at [LTX2Pipeline.swift:160](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/LTX2/LTX2Pipeline.swift:160), but audio substitutes zero when it is absent at [LTX2Pipeline.swift:275](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/LTX2/LTX2Pipeline.swift:275). Thus unseeded video varies while initial audio noise is identical across calls. Seeded server requests do preserve RNG isolation correctly.

10. **[MINOR] The tests permit a misleading green result.** The parity suites skip without machine-local weight extracts at [LTX2AVToplevelParityTests.swift:33](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Tests/ZImageTests/LTX2AVToplevelParityTests.swift:33), force weights/inputs to float32, and primarily compare correlation, which cannot detect gain/offset errors. The RoPE golden manually starts from raw coordinates at [LTX2AVToplevelParityTests.swift:106](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Tests/ZImageTests/LTX2AVToplevelParityTests.swift:106), bypassing the production seconds-valued grid; block parity supplies no positional embeddings at [LTX2AVBlockParityTests.swift:45](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Tests/ZImageTests/LTX2AVBlockParityTests.swift:45). The mux test uses 48 even frames—impossible for the generator’s `1+8k` contract—and allows 100ms audio-duration error at [LTX2AudioMuxTests.swift:39](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Tests/ZImageTests/LTX2AudioMuxTests.swift:39).

The scoped tests passed 12/12, including the separate `LTX2CallAVShapeTests`. I found no new row-order/table-slicing error or audio Euler sign error: `audio += (sigmaNext - sigma) * velocity` is correct for this flow parameterization.
