Verdict: the documents are not yet implementation-safe. The highest-risk issue is the shipped Phase 1 resolver: it reports one configuration while the renderer still consumes another. Prompt provenance also remains unjoinable across optimization, render, and Gallery.

Severity: Critical = can produce false/lost provenance; High = blocks a correct implementation or contaminates training data; Medium = underspecified behavior likely to diverge.

## Motion Tab + Prompt Lab

1. **[Critical] `output_path` cannot be the Gallery join key.** The spec links traces to assets by path ([motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:152](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/specs/motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:152)), but assets can move while retaining identity ([DAMAsset.swift:86](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ComfyBoxDesktop/DAM/DAMAsset.swift:86)), and the catalog already supports stable IDs, multiple locations, SHA deduplication, and `render_id` ([CatalogSchema.swift:40](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ComfyBoxCatalog/CatalogSchema.swift:40), [CatalogSchema.swift:153](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ComfyBoxCatalog/CatalogSchema.swift:153)). Assign a stable `render_id`/`artifact_id` before rendering, propagate it into trace and artifact metadata, and treat paths as mutable locators only.

2. **[High] Add `schema_version` and `task_kind` now.** The promised image follow-on “reuses this schema unchanged” ([motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:32](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/specs/motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:32)), but the only `mode` values are video-specific ([motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:43](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/specs/motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:43)); even current video has `storyboard` ([VideoTypes.swift:11](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/Video/VideoTypes.swift:11)). Put `schema_version` on traces and all three sidecar formats, and add a forward-compatible `task_kind` such as `video_render`, `image_render`, `img2img`, `inpaint`, or `storyboard`. Make `mode` task-specific or optional.

3. **[Critical] Terminal-only logging cannot log crashes.** The spec promises failed/crashed renders while writing only at terminal state ([motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:55](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/specs/motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:55), [motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:87](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/specs/motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:87)). A process crash has no terminal hook, and local-video jobs explicitly are not in durable queue persistence because they close over live state ([WarmServer.swift:4232](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/Server/WarmServer.swift:4232)). Use append-only lifecycle events—submitted/started/terminal—with a shared trace ID, then mark unfinished events `abandoned` during recovery. Serialize appends and rotation through one writer.

4. **[High] Shipped template provenance is not attached to a render and cannot reconstruct training input.** `PromptTemplateStore` computes only a 12-character digest ([PromptTemplateStore.swift:22](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/Telegram/PromptTemplateStore.swift:22)); `PromptOptimizer` emits it to an uncorrelated log line ([PromptOptimizer.swift:74](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/Telegram/PromptOptimizer.swift:74)), while `OptimizeResult` carries none of it ([PromptOptimizer.swift:14](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/Telegram/PromptOptimizer.swift:14)). The exporter nevertheless promises historical `{system, intent, final}` records ([motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:195](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/specs/motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:195)). Persist `template_id`, full digest, source, exemplar-set digest, and either the effective system text or a content-addressed immutable snapshot.

5. **[High] The exemplar hook targets a now-dead production path.** The spec says to modify `selectSystemPrompt` ([motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:184](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/specs/motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:184)), but production optimization now resolves through `PromptTemplateStore.shared.template` ([PromptOptimizer.swift:74](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/Telegram/PromptOptimizer.swift:74)); `selectSystemPrompt` remains only as legacy/test behavior ([PromptOptimizer.swift:392](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/Telegram/PromptOptimizer.swift:392)). Compose exemplars after file-over-builtin resolution and recompute the effective hash. Prefer separate user/assistant few-shot messages; injecting raw exemplar strings into the system prompt creates an instruction-injection boundary that length caps do not address.

6. **[High] Two client-supplied strings do not establish trustworthy optimization lineage.** The proposed `intent` and `optimized` fields ([motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:91](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/specs/motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:91)) can be stale, mismatched after “regenerate,” or fabricated, while `/v1/enhance` returns no attempt ID ([WarmServer.swift:2250](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/Server/WarmServer.swift:2250)). Return an `optimization_attempt_id` bound server-side to input, result, template, exemplars, and model; render submission should reference that ID.

7. **[High] Optimizer failure/refusal and `edited` semantics are undefined.** `edited = final != optimized` ([motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:47](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/specs/motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:47)) makes no definition for `optimized == null`, yet `edited` automatically enables promotion ([motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:178](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/specs/motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:178)). The current optimizer discards refusals and returns the raw prompt with `enhanced: false` ([PromptOptimizer.swift:88](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/Telegram/PromptOptimizer.swift:88)), while the desktop helper ignores `enhanced` and `note` entirely ([EngineService.swift:730](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ComfyBoxDesktop/EngineService.swift:730)). Add `optimizer_outcome`—skipped/succeeded/refused/timeout/error/fallback—and define `edited = optimized != nil && human_final != optimized`.

8. **[High] “Final” conflates the human-approved rewrite with the actual conditioning prompt.** After enhancement, the server may still prepend a character and apply preset prefix/suffix ([WarmServer.swift:1991](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/Server/WarmServer.swift:1991), [WarmServer.swift:2003](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/Server/WarmServer.swift:2003)). Persist both `human_final` and `render_prompt`; the former is the optimizer training target, while the latter is what rendered and should be used for render attribution.

9. **[High] “Automatic callers are unchanged” and “image logging is one hook” are false for current call paths.** Telegram currently optimizes before calling the engine ([ImageBotCoordinator.swift:1365](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/Telegram/ImageBotCoordinator.swift:1365)) and then submits only the final prompt ([ImageBotCoordinator.swift:1394](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/Telegram/ImageBotCoordinator.swift:1394)). The image follow-on therefore cannot recover intent, optimizer output, or template metadata from an engine hook. Audit every `PromptOptimizer.optimize` caller and carry an optimization-attempt reference through the render request.

10. **[High] The sealed/privacy contract is not end-to-end.** `log_trace:false` is specified only on render submission ([motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:105](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/specs/motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:105)); `/v1/enhance` has no privacy field ([WarmServer.swift:2184](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/Server/WarmServer.swift:2184)), nor does the current video request. Meanwhile the catalog already has a stronger `sealed` invariant that removes prompt text ([CatalogModels.swift:153](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ComfyBoxCatalog/CatalogModels.swift:153)). Use one `persistence_policy`/`sealed` value across optimization attempts, traces, artifacts, ratings, exemplars, export, and search.

11. **[High] Retention contradicts Gallery-only rating.** Unrated traces are pruned ([motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:77](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/specs/motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:77)), but assets without traces show no panel ([motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:158](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/specs/motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:158)); an older unrated Gallery asset therefore permanently loses the only place it can become rated. Preserve a minimal trace tombstone keyed by artifact ID, or keep traces while the artifact exists. Also define migration/coexistence with the Gallery’s existing scalar rating ([AssetDetailView.swift:325](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ComfyBoxDesktop/Views/AssetDetailView.swift:325)), including whether it counts as rated and what “strongly/well-rated” means.

12. **[High] Preference records do not identify what caused the preference.** The same `{winner, loser, batch, axis}` shape ([motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:68](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/specs/motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:68)) is reused for prompt-optimizer trials and renderer-parameter sweeps ([motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:191](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/specs/motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:191)). Feeding a parameter-driven win into DPO would label the prompt as causal when configuration was causal. Add `experiment_kind`, arm metadata, invariant hashes, varied factors, and a blinded comparison view that hides the prompt/config until after voting.

13. **[Medium] Source and storage ownership do not match the running system.** The closed source list expects `motion-tab`, but the desktop sends `"desktop"` and the server defaults to `"api"` ([EngineService.swift:1027](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ComfyBoxDesktop/EngineService.swift:1027), [WarmServer.swift:2072](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/Server/WarmServer.swift:2072)); consequently “own-authored” cannot be derived reliably. Split `client_surface`, `actor`, and `realm`, using open strings plus well-known values. Also specify which host owns the desktop-written files: the Gallery already presents assets whose bytes live on another server ([AssetMediaSource.swift:32](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ComfyBoxDesktop/DAM/AssetMediaSource.swift:32)), so local `~/.comfybox` sidecars need an API or explicit synchronization model.

## LTX-2 Parameter Externalization

14. **[Critical] Shipped Phase 1 is a split-brain readout, not effective configuration.** The resolver explicitly stops at config/env/builtin and defers request/preset ([LTX2ConfigResolver.swift:9](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/LTX2/LTX2ConfigResolver.swift:9)). The generator logs its output ([LTX2VideoGenerator.swift:555](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/LTX2/LTX2VideoGenerator.swift:555)), but the renderer still rereads raw environment values for compression, two-stage, rescale, decoding, sigmas, and other knobs ([LTX2VideoGenerator.swift:614](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/LTX2/LTX2VideoGenerator.swift:614), [LTX2Pipeline.swift:1028](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/LTX2/LTX2Pipeline.swift:1028)). Thus `config.json`, trimming, and validation can appear in the card/log while having no effect on the render. Make one typed `ResolvedVideoConfig` authoritative and pass it through every call site before relying on Phase 1 data anywhere.

15. **[High] The required per-job `resolved_config` did not ship.** Phase 1 requires it on video status ([ltx2-parameter-externalization.md:87](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/specs/ltx2-parameter-externalization.md:87)), but `VideoJobStatus` has no such field ([VideoTypes.swift:172](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/Video/VideoTypes.swift:172)), and `LocalVideoJob.toStatus` cannot supply it ([WarmServer.swift:4039](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/Server/WarmServer.swift:4039)). Jobs are also pruned after one hour ([WarmServer.swift:4179](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/Server/WarmServer.swift:4179)). Snapshot the authoritative config onto the job before execution and persist it into the trace.

16. **[High] The effective endpoint is neither hypothetical nor complete.** `GET /v1/video/config/effective` accepts no request or preset context ([WarmServer.swift:1148](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/Server/WarmServer.swift:1148)); the registry omits existing request fields such as guidance, strength, frames, dimensions, and FPS ([LTX2ConfigResolver.swift:52](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/LTX2/LTX2ConfigResolver.swift:52), [LTX2VideoGenerator.swift:32](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/LTX2/LTX2VideoGenerator.swift:32)). It also misses derived decisions such as aspect matching, snapping, two-stage halving, and duration folding ([WarmServer.swift:1885](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/Server/WarmServer.swift:1885), [WarmServer.swift:2008](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/Server/WarmServer.swift:2008)). Return both `requested_config` and the final `render_plan`, with provenance/transformation notes and the preset ID.

17. **[High] Validation behavior is internally inconsistent even before it becomes authoritative.** Any unknown Boolean string is accepted as valid `false` ([LTX2ConfigResolver.swift:153](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/LTX2/LTX2ConfigResolver.swift:153)); an invalid high-precedence value jumps directly to the builtin instead of trying a valid lower-precedence source ([LTX2ConfigResolver.swift:107](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/LTX2/LTX2ConfigResolver.swift:107)); and free-form sampler/decode strings receive no enum validation. Worse, `"true"` and padded `" 1 "` resolve as true but the renderer enables two-stage only for exact `"1"` ([WarmServer.swift:1926](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/Server/WarmServer.swift:1926)). Define strict parsers, fallback policy, typed values, and one canonical descriptor table.

18. **[High] `two_stage` is classified per-render but its dependency is load-time.** The upsampler is loaded only when `LTX2_TWO_STAGE == "1"` during model loading ([LTX2VideoGenerator.swift:462](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/LTX2/LTX2VideoGenerator.swift:462)); later render paths merely check whether that already-loaded object exists ([LTX2Pipeline.swift:1341](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/LTX2/LTX2Pipeline.swift:1341)). A Phase 2 request cannot switch false→true without a reload under the current lifecycle. Add a lifecycle column to the parameter manifest and either load the upsampler independently, lazy-load it on the first enabled request, or make relevant load-time values part of the generator cache key.

19. **[High] Phase 2 needs a field matrix, not a blanket “extend the structs” instruction.** Guidance, compression, strength, and frames already exist in the local request and MCP schema ([WarmServer.swift:1531](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/Server/WarmServer.swift:1531), [MCPToolRegistry.swift:451](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/MCP/MCPToolRegistry.swift:451)), while `ImagePreset` still has only generic image-era fields and an overloaded `mode` described as an executable/model mode ([PresetStore.swift:67](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/Server/PresetStore.swift:67)). Specify, per parameter: wire name, Swift type, legal range/enum, units, applicability, default, eligible precedence sources, lifecycle, preset serialization, MCP schema, UI control, and cross-field constraints. Tier B’s precedence should begin at config file rather than implying request/preset overrides.

20. **[High] Phase 3’s “matched” A/B is not specified strongly enough to remain matched.** The spec says only one seed and 2–4 values ([ltx2-parameter-externalization.md:123](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/specs/ltx2-parameter-externalization.md:123)). A batch must resolve once at submission and freeze prompt/template/exemplars, source-image hash, model/checkpoint, LoRAs, preset revision, and complete effective config; only the declared factor may differ. Define randomized arm order, batch/arm IDs, failure handling, and exact saturation/sharpness/flicker metrics.

## Fine-Tuned Prompt Optimizer PRD

21. **[High] `optimizer_model` must be added now, not when training starts.** The PRD explicitly proposes a later migration ([prd-prompt-optimizer-finetuning.md:98](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/specs/prd-prompt-optimizer-finetuning.md:98)), while the initial trace schema has only ambiguous `model` ([motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:51](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/specs/motion-tab-prompt-lab.md:51)). Add `renderer_model` and `optimizer_model` now, along with endpoint/provider and immutable revision/digest. Historical baseline data is already model-dependent.

22. **[High] “Only change the model name” contradicts the short-rubric goal.** The PRD says the system prompt becomes a short rubric ([prd-prompt-optimizer-finetuning.md:59](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/specs/prd-prompt-optimizer-finetuning.md:59)) but deployment says no engine change beyond the model name ([prd-prompt-optimizer-finetuning.md:94](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/specs/prd-prompt-optimizer-finetuning.md:94)). Current code always resolves and sends the externalized long template ([PromptOptimizer.swift:74](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/Telegram/PromptOptimizer.swift:74), [PromptOptimizer.swift:126](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/Sources/ZImage/Telegram/PromptOptimizer.swift:126)). Deploy an atomic optimizer profile containing model revision, tokenizer, adapter, template/rubric ID, generation settings, and refusal policy; rollback that profile as a unit.

23. **[High] The evaluation and data contracts cannot presently prove the stated goals.** The training record `{system,intent,final}` ([prd-prompt-optimizer-finetuning.md:37](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/specs/prd-prompt-optimizer-finetuning.md:37)) is not reproducible under mutable external templates and uses the confounded “actual render prompt” as its target. Store a frozen dataset manifest and train on `human_final`, not deterministic renderer additions. Group near-duplicate intents, variants, and A/B batches into one split before deduplication/retrieval so they cannot leak across train and held-out sets.

24. **[High] Retrieval-first and the hard goals are absent from the ship gate.** The PRD says retrieval must be exhausted first ([prd-prompt-optimizer-finetuning.md:71](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/specs/prd-prompt-optimizer-finetuning.md:71)), but evaluation compares only tuned versus prompted optimization ([prd-prompt-optimizer-finetuning.md:85](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/specs/prd-prompt-optimizer-finetuning.md:85)). Its ship rule requires only a downstream-render win and “no regression” on refusal/memory ([prd-prompt-optimizer-finetuning.md:92](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/specs/prd-prompt-optimizer-finetuning.md:92)), contradicting the goals of higher rule adherence and zero refusals. Compare incumbent, static exemplars, dynamic retrieval, SFT, and SFT+DPO; require explicit rule-adherence improvement, zero observed refusals on a pre-sized test set, and latency/memory bounds.

25. **[High] Alternating optimizers across ordinary production renders is not a causal A/B.** The deployment proposal ([prd-prompt-optimizer-finetuning.md:102](/Users/toddwalderman/Projects/zimage.swift/specs/prd-prompt-optimizer-finetuning.md:102)) confounds optimizer choice with changing intent, mode, source, parameters, and time. Use paired or randomized assignments for the same intent/config/seed, persist arm assignment before enqueue, blind the Gallery vote, and analyze by mode/content mode rather than pooling all traffic.

7. **#5, #12:** Integrate exemplars through the external template store and define typed, causally valid experiment records.
8. **#22:** Introduce versioned optimizer profiles so template/model deployment and rollback are atomic.

10. **#24–25:** Make retrieval a required baseline and replace alternating production traffic with paired, randomized, blinded evaluation.
